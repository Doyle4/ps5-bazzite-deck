## What's new
> **PRO ONLY.** A test build for the PS5 Pro. On an original PS5 or a Slim, stay on the release marked **Latest**; the fixes in here come to every model with the next full release.

- **Back to the display behaviour of v1.7.3:** 4K120, and 4K60 with VRR and HDR, stopped working in v1.8.1–v1.8.3 on a TV where they had worked. Those display changes are undone. Switching VRR on or off takes a couple of seconds of black screen again, as in v1.7.3.
- **PS5 Pro: no more error flood** on the screen and in the log (power controller "busy", CPU and GPU frequency changes failing): those two drivers no longer start on a Pro; its firmware keeps setting the clocks.
- The PS5 Pro support from v1.8.0 (GPU, its firmware, Bluetooth) stays.

## Testing
On a Pro: start with the loader twice, then post `ps5-report`, whether you get a picture, and `sudo dmesg | grep -i -e viola -e 64cu -e "clock control"`.

## Known issues
- **PS5 Pro:** untested beyond the first reports; tell us what works and what doesn't.
- **4K120 gives no picture on some TVs** while it works on others; being investigated.
- **GPU page faults when Game Mode starts** (coming from the desktop): the screen can freeze for a while.
