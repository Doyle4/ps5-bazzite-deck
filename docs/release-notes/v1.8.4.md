## What's new
> **PRO ONLY.** A test build for the PS5 Pro. On an original PS5 or a Slim, stay on the release marked **Latest**; the fixes in here come to every model with the next full release.

- **Fix: no picture at 4K120 on some TVs:** the GPU sent 4K120 to the HDMI chip in a format HDMI doesn't have (RGB at 6 bits per colour) instead of the one the chip's 4K120 setup is made for (YCbCr 4:2:2 at 8 bits). That also removes the colour banding 6 bits would have caused where it did show a picture.
- **Fix: no signal at every resolution after trying 4K120** on some TVs, until the TV was switched off. Going back from 4K120 to another mode now tells the TV that the 4K120 link is off, as a PC does.

- **PS5 Pro: no more error flood** on the screen and in the log (power controller "busy", CPU and GPU frequency changes failing): those two drivers no longer start on a Pro; its firmware keeps setting the clocks.

## Testing
On a TV where 4K120 gave no picture: select 4K120, wait, switch back to 4K60 (blind, or over SSH). The picture should come back without switching the TV off. Report with `sudo dmesg | grep -e "over FRL" -e "FRL off"` (the first line should now say `YCbCr 4:2:2, 8 bpc`) and your TV model.

## Known issues
- **4K120 gives no picture on some LG TVs** while it works on others; being investigated.
- **PS5 Pro:** first build (v1.8.0), untested on a Pro; tell us what works and what doesn't.
- **GPU page faults when Game Mode starts** (coming from the desktop): the screen can freeze for a while.
