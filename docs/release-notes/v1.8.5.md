## What's new
- **No more black screen from 4K120:** 4K120 is only offered when your TV announces the HDMI 2.1 link speed it needs, and if the 4K120 link still doesn't come up, the console switches back to 4K by itself within a few seconds and stops offering 4K120 for that TV. Before, a TV that couldn't take it stayed black until 4K120 was removed by hand. Based on Slip's work.
- `sudo dmesg | grep 4K120` says why 4K120 is or isn't offered, and whether its link came up.

## Testing
TVs where 4K120 worked: it should still work (`4K120 link up` in the log). TVs where it gave a black screen: pick 4K120 once; within about 8 seconds the picture should come back at 4K. Post `sudo dmesg | grep -e 4K120 -e "falling back"` and your TV model.

## Known issues
- **VRR and 4K120 on consoles with the VERDE HDMI chip** aren't supported yet, so they aren't offered there.
- **PS5 Pro:** a first version; tell us in #bazzite-ps5-pro-tests what works.
- **GPU page faults when Game Mode starts** (coming from the desktop): the screen can freeze for a while.
