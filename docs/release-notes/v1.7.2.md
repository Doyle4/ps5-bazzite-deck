## What's new
- **4K at 120 Hz** on consoles with the FLAVA3 HDMI chip and an HDMI 2.1 TV that supports it, over the TV's FRL link (Lawson's work, following Sony's own setup for this mode). Pick **3840x2160 at 120 Hz** in Game Mode under Settings → Display; it's offered when your TV supports 4K120. Between the GPU and the HDMI chip the picture runs as YCbCr 4:2:2 (the link can't carry 4K120 RGB); the TV gets real 4K120. VRR works at 4K120 too. You need an HDMI cable rated for 4K120.
- **VRR:** switching VRR or changing the resolution while the HDMI chip is still switching over no longer mixes the two setups.

## Known issues
- **No picture at 4K120 on your TV?** Wait a minute and switch back to 4K60. ` hdmi.frl_4k120=0` in `cmdline.txt` stops it being offered. Please report your TV model with `ps5-report` in #bazzite-problems.
- **VRR on consoles with the VERDE HDMI chip** isn't supported yet, so it isn't offered there (nor is 4K120).
- **GPU page faults when Game Mode starts** (coming from the desktop): the screen can freeze for a while. Being investigated.
- **Garbled picture in Game Mode on some older LG TVs with "HDMI Ultra Deep Colour" on:** switching Deep Colour off fixes it for now.
- **Steam downloads can get slower after switching to the desktop and back.** A cold start brings the speed back.
