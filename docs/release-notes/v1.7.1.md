## What's new
- **VRR:** on consoles with the FLAVA3 HDMI chip, VRR now works with TVs that support VRR or FreeSync, at 4K60, 1080p120, 1440p60 and 1440p120. Switch it on in Game Mode under Settings → Display → **Enable VRR**; it's off on a fresh install. After switching it on or changing the resolution, the screen stays dark for about 2 seconds while the HDMI chip switches over. At 4K60 the TV's range is usually 40–60 Hz, so below 40 fps it goes back to 60 Hz; at 120 Hz modes frame doubling covers lower frame rates.
- **Credit:** the fix is **Lawson's**: he found the missing timing packet on the GPU side. Thanks also to hsgodd for his captures of the stock system.
- **USB-C display output is off by default** (since v1.6.1), because switching it on can reset a boot drive on the front USB-C port. For a USB-C display or the PSVR2, add ` usbc_dp.enable=1` to `cmdline.txt`.
- **If you tested VRR before:** the update removes ` hdmi.vrr_test=1` from `cmdline.txt`; it isn't needed anymore.

## Known issues
- **VRR on consoles with the VERDE HDMI chip** isn't supported yet, so it isn't offered there (`ps5-report` shows which chip you have).
- **PS5 Pro:** not supported yet.
- **If VRR misbehaves with your TV:** switch it off in Steam, or add ` hdmi.vrr=0` to `cmdline.txt` so it's never offered, and tell us your TV model.
- **GPU page faults when Game Mode starts** (coming from the desktop): the screen can freeze for a while. Being investigated.
- **Garbled picture in Game Mode on some older LG TVs with "HDMI Ultra Deep Colour" on:** switching Deep Colour off fixes it for now.
- **Steam downloads can get slower after switching to the desktop and back.** A cold start brings the speed back.
