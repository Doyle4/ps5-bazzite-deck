## What's new
- **One DualSense for the PS5 and Linux** (thanks to **Dasloh**, PR #9): Linux uses its own Bluetooth address, so a DualSense keeps its PS5 pairing on one profile and the Linux pairing on another (for example PS + Square). No more re-pairing when you switch between the PS5 system and Linux. See [docs/dualsense-profiles.md](https://github.com/VivaLaVent/ps5-bazzite-deck/blob/main/docs/dualsense-profiles.md). On consoles with the MediaTek chip it's always on (pair your controllers with Linux once more after updating); on consoles with the IW620 chip (most original PS5s) it's off by default and `ps5-bt-address on` enables it.
- **Bluetooth that stays:** controllers reconnect by themselves when you press PS, pairing no longer gets undone a second later, and the Bluetooth adapter no longer vanishes after a while or after sleep (thanks to **Dasloh**, who found why, and to the ps5-cachyos-setup project for the pairing details).
- **Sleep:** a short press of the power button (or Steam's Sleep) puts the PS5 to sleep: screen off, orange light, game paused. A short press wakes it (one beep); game, network and Bluetooth continue. Thanks to **Dasloh** (PR #4 to the PS5 kernel patches).
- **TV remote (HDMI CEC):** the TV remote's arrows, OK and back work in Game Mode and on the desktop, and at boot the PS5 turns the TV on and switches it to its input (` ps5-cec.one_touch_play=0` in `cmdline.txt` stops that). Thanks to **Slip** (PR #53 to the PS5 kernel patches).
- **GPU load and temperature in the Steam performance overlay** (and MangoHud): they showed 0 before. The temperature is the chip's one sensor, so CPU and GPU show the same value; that's correct on the PS5, where both are one chip.
- **4K60 and 120 Hz back on TVs that had lost them** (issue #11): the PS5 now tells the TV it's an HDMI 2 source at every picture setup, so TVs that fell back to their HDMI 1.4 mode offer 4K60 and 120 Hz again.
- **Fewer black screens:** the link from the GPU to the PS5's HDMI chip always runs at full speed, and sleep no longer leaves the TV flickering in HDR.
- **Fast Wi-Fi:** power saving is off whenever Wi-Fi comes up; with it on, the IW620 throttles to ~100 Mbit/s instead of ~700 (thanks to thelonlybud for the tip).
- **USB never sleeps** (`usbcore.autosuspend=-1`, added to `cmdline.txt` by the image and the updater): fewer disconnects and freezes with USB drives and dongles.
- **Same IP after a reflash:** the console identifies to your router by its hardware address.
- **Restart straight back into Linux:** run `ps5-warm-reboot on` once, and every restart goes straight back into Linux in about 20 seconds, without the PS5 system and the loader. Wi-Fi is repaired automatically after one on IW620 consoles, and Bluetooth came back in testing too; if either is ever missing, power off and run the loader. Off by default because the PS5's hardware isn't reset by it: on some consoles the picture comes back garbled (Ctrl+Alt+F2, then Ctrl+Alt+F1 fixes it). `ps5-warm-reboot off` switches it off again.
- **Updating is one command**, for this and every future version (see below and the README).

## Known issues
- **First start:** after joining Wi-Fi, Steam updates itself behind a blank screen for several minutes. Don't power off there.
- **Garbled picture in Game Mode on some older LG TVs with "HDMI Ultra Deep Colour" on** (the desktop is fine). Switching Deep Colour off fixes it for now; being looked at.
- One report of no Wi-Fi on a PS5 Slim (12.20) is still open. If Wi-Fi won't connect on your console, send `journalctl -b --no-pager | grep -i -e wlan -e NetworkManager | grep -i -e fail -e error -e timeout | head -20` in Discord.
- **VRR isn't possible yet.** The PS5's HDMI chip has its own VRR mode (found by **hsgodd** in the stock system), but TVs still drop the signal with it; work continues in separate test builds.
- If the picture blanks every few seconds on a monitor with a "console mode", try ` ps5-cec.one_touch_play=0` in `cmdline.txt` and tell us whether it helps.
