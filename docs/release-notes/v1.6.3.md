## What's new
- **VRR works at every mode now,** 1440p60 and 1440p120 included (FLAVA3 consoles, with ` hdmi.vrr_test=1` in `cmdline.txt`; Steam's VRR setting switches it).
- **Cleaner, faster switching:** when VRR goes on or the mode changes, the screen stays dark for about 2 seconds while the HDMI chip switches over, instead of showing a drifting or flickering picture for longer.

## VRR test
For **FLAVA3** consoles with a TV that supports VRR or FreeSync: ` hdmi.vrr_test=1` in `cmdline.txt`, cold start, then Settings → Display → **Enable VRR** in Game Mode. Lower a game's frame limit and watch the TV's VRR or FreeSync info follow it. Expect about 2 seconds of black screen after switching VRR on or changing the mode.

**Report:** `vrr-report`, in #bazzite-prereleases-experimental.

## Known issues
- **GPU page faults when Game Mode starts** (coming from the desktop): the screen can freeze for a while. Being investigated.
- **Garbled picture in Game Mode on some older LG TVs with "HDMI Ultra Deep Colour" on:** switching Deep Colour off fixes it for now.
- **Steam downloads can get slower after switching to the desktop and back.** A cold start brings the speed back.
