# v1.8 plan: PS5 Pro, ALLM, small fixes

## ALLM — kernel done, needs testing
`patches/ps5-hdmi-allm.patch` (on 1.7.3): stock's HDMI Forum VSIF with ALLM_Mode in the FLAVA3 chip's vendor
InfoFrame slot, after every setup (TMDS and FRL), when the TV announces ALLM. `hdmi.allm=0` switches it off.
**Test:** on an LG/Samsung/Sony with ALLM, the TV switches to its game mode by itself at boot and after mode changes;
VRR and 4K120 keep working; nothing changes on a TV without ALLM.

## PS5 Pro — needs a Pro owner who tests regularly

**Part one done (kernel and firmware, untested):** `patches/ps5-pro-gpu.patch` (upstream #58 by Borchev, ported to 7.3:
all hunks apply, the golden-settings table from upstream's post-#58 hunk, the SMU condition ported by hand; compiles)
and `files/ps5-stage-viola-fw` (+ `.service`): at boot, if the loader dumped all 8 Viola files to the boot partition,
appends them to `initrd.img` as an extra initramfs archive (original kept as `initrd.img.noviola`, redone after an
update replaced the initrd). Nothing happens on other consoles. Check on a Pro: `journalctl -u ps5-stage-viola-fw`,
then after the next start `sudo dmesg | grep -i -e viola -e "PS5 Pro" -e "64cu"`.
1. **GPU** (upstream #58, `f21f65b`): Viola golden settings, 64 CUs, SMU backend, Pro sensors. Written against 7.2.3:
   port to our 7.3 tree. It switches itself off without the Pro's GPU firmware, so it's safe on other consoles.
   Files: `amdgpu.h`, `amdgpu_discovery.c`, `amdgpu_ucode.c/.h`, `gfx_v10_0.c` (+134), `amdgpu_pm.c`,
   `cyan_skillfish_ppt.c` (+164), `drivers/ps5/mp1.c` (+340, 3-way merge like `hdmi.c`), `drivers/ps5/thermal.c`

   before/after of the PS5 files).
2. **GPU firmware:** the loader dumps 8 Viola files to the boot partition. Upstream rebuilds the initrd on the console

3. **Wi-Fi:** MediaTek MT7925 (`14c3:7925`): `CONFIG_MT7925E` and its firmware.
4. **Bluetooth:** MediaTek `0e8d:3605`, QuartConz's patch (preview), incl. the udev rule against USB autosuspend.
5. **Loader:** 11.xx–13.xx Pro payloads exist (dl21 / rmux PR #69).
**Steps 3 and 4 done too (untested):** `CONFIG_MT7925E=m` is already in our kernel config; `patches/ps5-pro-bluetooth.patch`

## PS5 Pro: findings from others (Nonu, 26 Oct)
- **60 CUs = 30 WGPs** is the Pro's full set, enabled with a corrected CU/WGP mask (registers
  `CC_GC_SHADER_ARRAY_CONFIG`, `SPI_PG_ENABLE_STATIC_WGP_MASK`, `RLC_PG_ALWAYS_ON_WGP_MASK`). Upstream #58 (our port)
  requests 30 active WGPs from the SMU, which matches; but its `amdgpu.ps5_cu_unlock` path on the Pro uses WGP mask
  `0xff` (8 per shader array, 64 CUs), more than exist. So: no `ps5_cu_unlock` on a Pro (README says so); a kernel
  guard that ignores it on Viola would be safer.
- **SMU mailbox:** base PS5 message `0x03B10A08`, argument `0x03B10A48`, response `0x03B10A68`; the Pro's mapping is
  unknown. That fits the "busy" flood from our PS5 clock drivers on the Pro (v1.8.4 switches them off there). Pro clock
  control needs that mapping first.
- **GameOS `/dev/mp1` ioctls:** `0x4068AE01` GET_CLK, `0x8004AE17` SET_GFXCLK, `0xC004AE18` REQUEST_MODE_SWITCH,
  `0x8008AE1D` SET_COREPSTATE, `0xC00CAE1E` GET_COREPSTATE.
- Not yet validated by anyone: full-CU compute performance and Vulkan stability on the Pro.
